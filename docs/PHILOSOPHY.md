# Philosophy of Color

## 5 Philosophical Schools

### 1. Newton (1666) - The Spectrum
White light splits into spectrum via prism.

### 2. Goethe (1810) - Perception
Color is subjective - the eye shapes it.

### 3. Munsell (1905) - The System
Hue . Value . Chroma.

### 4. Itten (1961) - 7 Contrasts
Bauhaus theory.

### 5. OKLCH (2020) - Perceptual Uniformity
Equal numeric changes = equal perceptual changes.

## 8 Golden Rules

1. 60-30-10 - Color distribution
2. Contrast - Minimum 4.5:1
3. Harmony - Complementary, triadic, analogous
4. Temperature - Balance warm and cool
5. Saturation - High grabs attention
6. Lightness - Vary for hierarchy
7. Accessibility - Never rely on color alone
8. Cultural - Respect East/West differences
